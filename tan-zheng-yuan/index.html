Chapter II

Hero characters

Five game-ready adventurers, each taken through a refinement pass in Unreal Engine 5.8 and exported back to real time. Pick one to inspect.

Refinement pass

The Knight

A CC0 game character, imported into an isolated Unreal Engine 5.8 project and upgraded with a scripted look-dev and optimisation pass. The same scripts handle all five heroes.

  • Material pass: new Unreal materials re-grade every swatch of the palette into PBR: polished steel, gunmetal plates, dark leather, violet house cloth, gold trim and a violet enamel shield.
  • Rune-lit blade: the sword moved to its own material slot with an emissive glow.
  • One skeletal mesh: loadout trimmed to sword and badge shield; helmet, cape and props skinned into the rig.
  • Beauty render: a studio scene built and lit by script inside Unreal, captured as raw HDR and graded (the UE render view).
  • LOD chain: 100% / 50% / 25% of the triangles, generated with Unreal’s skeletal mesh reduction, then baked and exported to glTF with four animations.

Base models: KayKit Adventurers by Kay Lousberg (CC0). Refined, reduced, exported and rendered in Unreal Engine 5.8.

The Realm

A living hex world: mountains, a river, a lake and the open sea, farmland, forests, a castle and its village. A dragon hunts on the wing, wild horses run the plain and a skeleton host marches on the ford. Every model was sourced under CC0, then assembled, re-graded and exported in Unreal Engine 5.8.
